## 1. Summary

The operator today front-funds salaries and recovers capital + fee later (per-timesheet `revenue_receivable`, a % service fee). The new app drops money movement entirely: salaries are paid externally from the paying company's own bank account, the platform records who was paid in each Kỳ (pay cycle), counts distinct employees per Kỳ by CCCD, and bills a flat per-employee-per-Kỳ fee at month end. One deployment, one database — no customer/partner entity exists at all (D21). The platform's terminal deliverable is the monthly billing report export; collecting the fee is manual, outside the system (D9).

```
Customer pays salaries ──► uploads bank transfer result ──► platform verifies
       ▲                                                          │   reject ─► push all admins (D10)
       │                                                          ▼
Month-end billing report ◄── distinct employees (CCCD) × fee ◄── per-Kỳ count
       │
       ▼
Operator sends report ──► payment happens outside the system
```

## 2. Problem

Front-funding is capital-intensive and credit-exposed, and it forces the platform to run payment rails: 9Pay/OnePay disbursement (`backend/internal/infra/disbursement/ninepay/`), FlexPay advances (`backend/internal/app/services/flex_pay/`, `advance_payment/`), wallet + double-entry ledger (`backend/internal/domain/wallet/`, `domain/ledger.go`), loans (`domain/loan.go`), bulk transfer file generation (`backend/internal/app/services/payroll/bulktransfer/`). None of this is needed when the customer funds salaries.

## 3. Goals

| # | Goal |
|---|---|
| G1 | Zero platform-held funds: no gateway, no wallet, no disbursement, no advances |
| G2 | Fee revenue derived fully from usage data: distinct employees × Kỳ × price |
| G3 | Month-end billing report generated by the system, not hand-computed |
| G4 | Schema rebuilt for the new model; every removed-feature table dropped, not migrated |
| G5 | One deployment, one database — no customer/partner entity, no multi-tenant scoping (D21) |

## 4. Monthly Lifecycle

1. Salaries are paid per Kỳ externally (the paying company's own bank account).
2. The paying company uploads the bank transfer result for that Kỳ.
3. Platform verifies the upload (cross-check against the Kỳ's approved timesheets) and marks the covered timesheets customer-paid; on mismatch, all admins receive a push notification and the Kỳ count stays frozen (D10).
4. Platform counts distinct employees (CCCD) for the Kỳ from the verified, customer-paid timesheets.
5. Counts accumulate across the month's 4 Kỳ.
6. After Kỳ 4 (month end): system exports the consolidated billing report (Excel) — `giamdoc` generates/exports it (D19) and the operator sends it out to request payment. Payment collection is out of scope (D9).

## 5. Business Rules

### 5.1 Canonical Kỳ Calendar (already canonical in code; fixed by decision D8)

Source: `backend/internal/pkg/clock/pay_cycle.go` — `TimesheetKyCount = 4`, `kyWorkStartDay = {0,1,8,15,22}`, `kyPayDayInMonth = {0,10,17,24,0}`, `KyFromWorkDay`, `PayDate`, `TimesheetPayCycle.CycleWindow()`.

| Kỳ | Work window | Pay date |
|---|---|---|
| Kỳ 1 | Day 1 – Day 7 | 10th of the month |
| Kỳ 2 | Day 8 – Day 14 | 17th of the month |
| Kỳ 3 | Day 15 – Day 21 | 24th of the month |
| Kỳ 4 | Day 22 – end of month | 1st of the following month |

### 5.2 Pricing Rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-1 | Fee = X VND per distinct employee per Kỳ; default X = 10,000 VND; admin-configurable in Settings (flat fee — replaces the donor % model on `Timesheet.RevenueReceivable`) |
| BR-2 | "Distinct" = unique CCCD within one Kỳ (one deployment = one billable entity, D21); same employee in N Kỳ bills N × X. CCCD is globally unique, hard-enforced by `UNIQUE(cccd)` (no soft delete, D16) |
| BR-3 | A Kỳ's count is included only after its transfer result is uploaded and verified (the customer-paid marker is the only counting gate) |
| BR-4 | The §5.1 calendar is the only definition of "round"/Kỳ — fixed for the deployment (D8). The donor's per-project monthly period overrides (`Project.SalaryPeriodFrom/To`) are not carried over |

Worked example: 4 Kỳ × 50 distinct employees × 10,000 VND = 2,000,000 VND/month.

## 7. Data Model Implications

- Keep: employees, projects, project_employees, timesheets, users/auth/audit, notifications, settings (reduced key set). No soft-delete columns anywhere — deletes are hard DELETEs (D16). No effective-to columns/logic anywhere — effective-from only (D17).
- New tables: per-Kỳ paid-records read model (FR-1); transfer uploads with content hash + verification state (FR-2, settlement-upload pattern); billing reports with fee snapshots (FR-3/4). No `customers` table and no customer/partner entity — one deployment is one billable entity (D21).
- Dropped Project columns: `salary_period_from/to` (D8), `is_flexible`, `geofence_gates`, `shift_names` (D12).
- Employee `BankID`/`BankAccount*` fields and the OnePay account-verification status (`BankAccountStatus`) lose their consumer once disbursement dies — drop with the schema rebuild unless payroll export needs bank details (appendix).
- Drop (gorm entities in `backend/internal/domain/`): `ad_banner`, `advance_payment*`, `api_key`, `mcp_api_key`, `ops_api_key`, `api_endpoint`, `api_metric`, `loan*`, `wallet/*`, `ledger`, `transaction*`, `settlement*`, `bulk_transfer_*`, `cron_job_status`, `disbursement_fee_schedule`, `weekly_payment_fee_schedule`, `employee_paid_activity` (read model of the removed export; its per-employee aggregation pattern is the FR-1 template).

## 8. Decision Log

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | Who funds salaries? The customer; the platform counts only after the transfer result is uploaded and verified. |
| D2 | What is a "round"? The fixed 4-Kỳ calendar in §5.1 (same calendar the codebase already uses). |
| D3 | Who is a distinct employee in a Kỳ? Unique by CCCD. |
| D4 | Who is billed and how? The customer, monthly: system-generated report of distinct employees per Kỳ × fee (default 10,000 VND/employee/Kỳ). |
| D5 | Fee is flat per head per Kỳ (BR-1), not a percentage of transfers — founder's explicit choice, replacing the donor's `RevenueReceivable` % model. |
| D6 | Counting substrate is timesheets, not raw upload rows — uploads verify against approved timesheets (FR-2); dedupe by CCCD comes free from the employee registry. |
| D7 | Tenancy: single-partner schema — one deployment serves one customer (founder decision 2026-11-19). No `customers` table; CCCD uniqueness stays global; `partner_company` setting names the customer. |
| D8 | The 4-Kỳ calendar is fixed for the deployment — no per-customer or per-project configuration (founder decision 2026-11-19). `Project.SalaryPeriodFrom/To` overrides die with the schema rebuild. |
| D9 | Fee collection is out of scope (founder decision 2026-11-19): the platform's terminal deliverable is the monthly billing report export; the operator sends it to the customer and collects payment manually. No payment integration, no invoice/VAT module. |
| D10 | Rejected/incorrect transfer upload (founder decision 2026-11-19): the upload is marked rejected, the Kỳ count stays frozen until a corrected upload passes verification, and a push notification is sent to all admins. |
| D11 | Paid-marker semantics (engineering decision 2026-11-19, founder notified): one marker — `payment_status='paid'` + `payment_date` mean "customer-paid, verified from upload"; only FR-2 verification writes them; no separate `customer_paid_at` field; the old platform-disbursement meaning is not carried into the rebuilt schema. |
| D12 | Check-in config columns die with self check-in (founder decision 2026-11-19): `Project.IsFlexible`, `GeofenceGates`, `ShiftNames` are all removed in the rebuild. |
| D13 | The entire Sổ cái (ledger page) is removed — page, route, nav, and all actions (founder requirement 2026-11-19, upgraded from actions-only): the new model has no ledger; counting comes from verified customer uploads (D6). |
| D14 | Front-fund accounting surfaces are removed (founder requirement 2026-11-19): all 2%-style percentage fee logic and the accounting dashboard widgets (Hiệu quả kinh doanh, Thanh khoản, Phải thu, Phải trả, Vay nợ, Vốn chủ sở hữu) — ledger aggregates with no meaning once the ledger dies; revenue visibility comes from the FR-3 billing report instead. |
| D15 | The attendance module is removed entirely (founder requirement 2026-11-19): no `attendances`/`attendance_failed_attempts` tables, no attendance flows — timesheets (admin entry + BCC import) are the only salary record source. |
| D16 | No soft delete anywhere (founder requirement 2026-11-19): every delete is a hard DELETE. `deleted_at` columns, `gorm.DeletedAt`, and soft-delete-conditional indexes are removed; uniqueness constraints become plain UNIQUE (e.g. `UNIQUE(cccd)` instead of unique-among-active-rows); `audit_logs` remains the record of what was deleted. |
| D17 | Effective-from only (founder requirement 2026-11-19): every effective-from/effective-to pair collapses to a single effective-from.
